# Amendment record — J2, untitled-recovery-document over-cap state

**Ratified by CJ 2026-08-27; revised and re-ratified 2026-08-28. Applied by PR #1833 / issue #1421**, against `main` at `f24dc462ae77`. The authoritative wording lives in `docs/ux-design.md` (J2) and, where it touches requirements, `docs/functional-spec.md`; PR #1833 applies it there. This file is the record — a reference copy, not an authority.

**It supersedes the wording ratified 2026-08-26** (which assumed the withdrawn read-only pane) and **amends the 2026-08-27 text** on the seven points ratified 2026-08-28: the live/restored over-cap split, the honest override, current-state empty-guard wording, persistence proof before an unguarded close, one logical writer per session, FR-X-11 precedence, and durable adoption provenance as an event with a D15 projection.

**Why the revision was needed.** The over-cap wording ratified by CJ on **2026-08-26** described a **read-only** recovery pane with no Save, no Save As, and a confirmed-close-only exit. That lifecycle no longer exists: the **#1379 untitled/scratch document amendment, ratified by CJ 2026-08-21** and shipped by PR #1805, makes the recovery document **editable**, keeps autosave off by construction, makes `[Save As…]` the ratified adoption route, and gives close the three-action guard (`[Save As…]` / `[Discard]` / `[Cancel]`). `RecoveredDocument` is deleted; `UntitledDocument` is the pane. **That conflict is settled.** The 2026-08-26 wording had been ratified five days after the lifecycle it assumed was superseded; CJ resolved it on **2026-08-27** in favour of the text below, which replaces it.

**Where it lands:** `docs/ux-design.md`, J2, immediately after the **Untitled/scratch document lifecycle (D12, FR-ED-8, #1379)** paragraph.

**What it supplies.** J2 as amended by #1379 recorded the editable lifecycle, but not: the over-cap notice; the persistent amber `UNTITLED` tab state; `[Hide message]` / `[Details]`; durable persistence of the outcome and the hidden flag across quit and relaunch; the empty-buffer body of the close guard; or what becomes of the omission on successful adoption.

## Ratified insertion (as applied by PR #1833)

> Recovery content is held inline in the session record, subject to a per-tab limit (2 MiB). The limit governs what the session can keep, not what the author has: a buffer above it is still present and editable in the window that holds it, and Emend says so — that the work is here, that the session cannot keep it, and that saving it into the project is the only way to keep it. Only after a relaunch the session could not carry is the work gone, and only then does Emend say it could not be preserved, that it exceeded the per-tab limit, that it cannot be restored, and that the document is not in the project yet. These two are never told as one thing; where both are true of one document, the still-actionable risk is stated first and the earlier loss separately, and neither is written so that saving reads as recovering the other.
>
> The document is editable and adoptable exactly as any other untitled document is, and nothing in either state presents editing or saving as recovery of work that was not kept, or offers a retry, a compare, or any other affordance implying it can be reached (Principle 7).
>
> A restored omission is a standing fact about the tab for as long as the tab is untitled. Editing does not clear it, and while the document is untitled there is no save that could. The author may hide the explanation; hiding collapses it to a persistent statement, never presents the state as resolved, and returns as hidden after a relaunch. The live warning is not hidden at all: it is a risk the author can still act on.
>
> Two facts are persisted with the session and survive quit and relaunch: that a tab's recovery content was omitted for exceeding the per-tab limit, and whether the author has hidden the explanation. Neither can be reconstructed from the recovery path and the absence of content, so the outcome is recorded explicitly.
>
> Closing an untitled document always asks. What it asks depends on what closing would actually destroy: work that is only here, work the session cannot keep, work written since an earlier loss, or nothing at all — and where it would destroy nothing, it says so rather than warning about work that no longer exists, and discarding is not presented as destructive. Every one of these offers saving, discarding and cancelling, in that order, with cancelling the default and dismissal cancelling.
>
> Quitting does not ask about an untitled document only when Emend has been told, authoritatively, that every remaining untitled document was kept in full. A write reporting success while having dropped a buffer is not that assurance; where any buffer was not kept, Emend asks about it rather than closing. A run still in progress refuses the close before any of this begins — nothing is decided, staged, or written — and the author closes again once it has stopped.
>
> Quitting resolves the window's dirty project documents first, one at a time. Those decisions take effect only when the session is written: a document saved during the queue is saved, but its tab stays open until the window does; a document the author chose to discard is recorded and nothing is thrown away yet. Emend writes the session it would have after all of those closes, in a single write it waits for, and only then are the closes carried out. Where the write fails, none of them are, and the plan is kept so the author can try again or quit anyway. Where the session Emend would write is not meaningfully different from the one it last saved, it does not write again, and it never says anything is at risk.
>
> Where the write fails, Emend stays open with every tab intact, gives the actual reason, says no new session was committed, and says what would come back if the author quit anyway — the previous saved session, none at all, or something it could not verify. Quitting anyway abandons the session changes and closes; it does not pretend to have carried out the closes durably, and it discloses that the older session, including tabs the author closed or earlier versions of open documents, may reopen. Remaining open is the default. Where more than one untitled document is at risk, Emend says how many and names them all.
>
> When an untitled document is adopted into the project, the fact that earlier work was not part of what was saved is recorded durably before the adoption is made, and shown afterwards in the conversation record. Where it is saved but that record has not yet been written, Emend says so plainly, and completes the record later — once, however many times it tries.
>
> These guarantees are for one window's close request. Quitting the application asks each participating window, any of which may refuse. Nothing is guaranteed where the application is terminated rather than asked to quit.

## What this does and does not change

**Does not change:** that a recovery document is created only for a *dirty* displaced tab; the #1379 editable lifecycle, its autosave-off-by-construction rule, and its never-dirty tab; `[Save As…]` as the ratified adoption route and its unchanged confined save panel; the three-action close guard as the only exit; that cancelling or failing Save As changes nothing; that no rail row is marked active while it is the active tab; the moved document's ownership of the original path; the 2 MiB cap itself.

**Does change:** the override is `[Quit anyway]` — it **abandons the current session delta and closes**, claims no durable commit, and discloses that previous state may reopen; it records the over-cap state J2 is silent on; fixes the honesty requirement (four facts: not preserved, over the per-tab limit, not restorable, not in the project yet); fixes the persistence rule (editing does not clear it; hiding is not resolving); states that adoption ends the state, and that **no document-attached provenance field survives adoption — the durable post-adoption record is a machine-local adoption event keyed by a stable `adoptionId`**, of which the D15 timeline card is a reconstructible projection (the card itself is new work; current `main` has none), and whose appended sentence is an implementation requirement; adds a **durable-persistence requirement** on the tab-session record for the over-cap outcome and the hidden/expanded flag — two fields, not a derivation, with consequences in `src-tauri/src/store/tab_session.rs` and `src/stores/tabs.ts`; makes the final session write on quit **awaited**, with an honest stay-open/retry outcome on failure (the no-guard-on-quit rule depends on it); and bounds migration to the unambiguous legacy shape only. The legacy `recovered_from` + `unsaved_content` pair remains **migration-only**, and a record already normalized to `unsaved_content: Some("")` is **not** migrated — it is indistinguishable from a genuinely empty buffer and must not receive a possibly false loss warning. That is a recorded limitation with a stated disposition, not a gap.

**Five guard variants, ratified as drawn.** `only-copy`, `live-over-cap`, `omitted-with-edits`, `restored-omitted-empty` and `preserved-empty`. The first three are destructive; the last two are not, because closing them destroys nothing. The empty bodies use **current-state wording** ("empty now"), which stays true after type-then-delete, and `preserved-empty` carries **no omission copy** while still raising the guard. All five keep `[Save As…]` / `[Discard]` / `[Cancel]`, in that order, with `Cancel` the default.

**Post-adoption provenance: no *document-attached* record; a durable adoption event with a D15 projection.** The adopted document carries no provenance field of its own. The durable record is a **machine-local adoption event keyed by a stable `adoptionId`**, persisted before the irreversible boundary and phased `prepared` → `file-committed` → `published`, with `cancelled`/`tombstoned` as terminal; **only `file-committed` events may project**. The D15 timeline card is that event's **reconstructible projection**, not the record — and **current `main` has no scratch-adoption card of any kind**, so both the card and an awaited, confirmed append are new work. Publication is retried idempotently across relaunch, deduplicated by `adoptionId`. See `specs/adoption-provenance.md`.

**Out of scope:** no retroactive classification of ambiguous legacy sessions; no change to the cap; no new UXR identifier (`UXR-Q12` belongs to J11 dismissal/reopen work); no production code from this folder. The two persisted fields *are* in scope.
