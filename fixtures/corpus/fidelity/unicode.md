# Unicode & typographic fidelity

Smart quotes: “curly double” and ‘curly single’, plus an apostrophe’s tail.
Dashes: an en–dash and an em—dash, and a minus−sign. Ellipsis…

Accents and scripts: café, naïve, Þorn, Ærø, Москва, 東京, العربية, עברית.

Combining marks: e vs é (composed) vs é (e + combining acute). Zero-width
joiner sequences: 👩‍💻 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 🏳️‍🌈. Skin-tone modifiers: 👍🏽.

Whitespace variety: a non-breaking space[ ]here, and a tab→	←there.
